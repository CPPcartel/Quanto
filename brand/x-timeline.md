# Quanto launch timeline: 20 posts

Ordered for posting, not for reading. Times are US Eastern because the market
open is the single best asset you have and everything is scheduled around it.

**Voice notes.** Lowercase by default, capitals only for tickers and emphasis. No
em dashes anywhere in this file, by request. Short lines. No "excited to
announce", no "we are thrilled", no rocket emojis, no "gm ser". CT stopped
rewarding that years ago. What still works: specific numbers, dry delivery, and
showing the thing instead of describing it.

**The hard rule.** Not one of these posts promises money, and none of them should
be edited to. $BLOCK has no cash value and no cash out. Degen tone, honest
claims. That combination is rarer than either one alone and it is the actual
differentiator.
